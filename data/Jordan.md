# Jordan 🇯🇴 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
World Cup debutants in 2026. Jordan has been improving steadily in AFC competition over the past decade — reaching the 2023 Asian Cup final (lost to Qatar) was a breakthrough result. Their AFC qualifying run included a famous victory over South Korea, one of Asia's traditional powers. The WC qualification is the greatest achievement in Jordanian football history.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.0 avg attack / 1.5 defense in qualifying. Defense concedes more than ideal; attack relies on individual moments from Al-Taamari.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Limited — Jordan is not a set-piece specialist.
- **Style:** Organized defensive block, physical, counter-attacking when opportunities arise.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Musa Al-Taamari (Stade Rennais, RW — captain):** The first Jordanian to play regularly in Ligue 1. Creative, tricky, dangerous in one-vs-one. The sole genuine European-quality player.
- **Yazan Al-Naimat (Al-Jazira, ST):** Domestic scorer, limited exposure to elite opposition.
- **Mohammad Abu-Laila (Al-Wehdat, CM):** Experienced domestic midfielder providing leadership.
- **Tactic:** 5-4-1 or 4-5-1 defensive block. Direct to Al-Taamari on transitions. Minimize conceding against Argentina and Austria.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Argentina:** No WC history. Containing Messi for as long as possible is the first-half ambition.
- **vs Austria:** The best competitive fixture. Al-Taamari vs Laimer/Sabitzer is a genuine contest.
- **vs Algeria:** The most winnable match in Group J. Both sides at lower Elo levels.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Jordan's historic qualification deserves celebration. In Group J with Argentina, Austria, and Algeria, they face a very tough draw. Realistically, group stage exit is expected. The target: beat Algeria, take a point from Austria, make Argentina work hard. Al-Taamari's Ligue 1 quality is their only European-level talent — if he has a tournament of his life, Jordan can surprise Algeria. Every Jordan goal will be celebrated across the Arab world.
