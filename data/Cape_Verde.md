# Cape Verde 🇨🇻 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
World Cup debutants in 2026 — a historic achievement for the island archipelago with ~560,000 people. Cape Verde won their CAF qualifying group ahead of Senegal and Nigeria, which shocked the continent. Their AFCON performances have been increasingly competitive — reaching the quarter-finals of AFCON 2023 and 2024. The WC qualification is their greatest sporting achievement.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.1 avg attack / 1.5 defense in qualifying. Defensive record is manageable; scoring remains limited.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Gelson Martins' ability to drive into the box creates second-ball opportunities. Physical approach on corners.
- **Style:** Physically imposing, direct, play on the counter. Similar profile to Senegal 10 years ago.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Gelson Martins (Monaco, RW — captain):** The undisputed star. Electric pace, Ligue 1 regular, can beat defenders in one-vs-one situations. If he plays a perfect game, Cape Verde can upset anyone.
- **Ryan Mendes (veteran, LW):** Long-time Cape Verde stalwart — experience and leadership.
- **Stopira (Al-Ahli, CB):** Defensive organizer with Gulf League experience.
- **Tactic:** 4-5-1 / 4-4-2 defensive block. Direct to Martins and Mendes on transitions.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Spain:** No realistic path to a result here. Contain damage.
- **vs Uruguay:** More competitive. Physical battle where Cape Verde can compete.
- **vs Saudi Arabia:** The key target match — similar level teams. A win here would secure group stage credibility.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Cape Verde's story is the WC's best qualification narrative outside of Haiti. They won a group containing Senegal and Nigeria, two Top-20 African football nations. Gelson Martins is a legitimate Ligue 1 quality winger. Their realistic target: beat Saudi Arabia, take a point from Uruguay, give Spain a hard game in the first 45 minutes. Group stage exit is likely but this team will fight for every minute.
