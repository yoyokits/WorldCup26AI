## Group A

### Argentina 🇦🇷 — FIFA #1 · CONMEBOL
- **World Cup appearances:** 10 (first: 1930)
- **Best result:** Champions (1978, 1986, 2022) | Runner-up (1930, 1990, 2014)
- **Last WC:** 🏆 Winner 2022 (Qatar) — beat France in final
- **Recent form:** Copa America 2024 Champions. Dominant possession-based side.
- **Key players:** Lionel Messi (PSG/Inter Miami, captain, playmaker), Julián Álvarez (Man City, forward), Lautaro Martínez (Inter Milan, striker), Emiliano Martínez (Aston Villa, GK), Alexis Mac Allister (Liverpool, CM)
- **Strengths:** Unmatched attacking talent, set-piece threat, Messi's leadership. Solid midfield control with Mac Allister + De Paul.
- **Weaknesses:** Aging core around Messi (37). Transition defense can be vulnerable to fast counters.
- **Match analysis notes:** Favorite in Group A. Likely to dominate possession. Watch for Messi's role — may be managed carefully. Alvarez/Martinez partnership is key if Messi drops deeper.

### Jamaica 🇯🇲 — FIFA #20 · CONCACAF (DEBUT)

### Morocco 🇲🇦 — FIFA #18 · CAF

## How to Use This Document for Match Analysis

### Strength Comparison Matrix
When analyzing any matchup, consider:
1. **FIFA Ranking gap** — >10 positions = significant quality difference
2. **Physical vs Technical** — Which style wins? (e.g., Nigeria's pace vs Japan's technique)
3. **Set-piece threat** — Teams like Australia, Morocco are dangerous on corners/free-kicks
4. **Home/away advantage** — Host nations (USA, Mexico, Canada) get crowd support
5. **WC experience** — Teams like Spain, Germany have knockout DNA; debutants like Jamaica don't

### Key Matchup Patterns to Watch
- **Pace vs Physicality:** Ghana/Senegal vs Japan/Korea — who wins the aerial duel?
- **Possession vs Counter:** Spain/Brazil vs Tunisia/Turkey — can the counter beat the press?
- **Star Power vs Team Play:** Argentina (Messi) vs Croatia (Modric) — individual brilliance or collective?
