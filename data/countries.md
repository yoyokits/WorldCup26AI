# World Cup 2026 — All 48 Teams: Profiles & Analysis

> Living document. Updated: 2026-06-10 (Tournament starts June 11)
> Official draw: December 5, 2025 — JFK Center for the Performing Arts, Washington D.C.
> Format: 48 teams, 12 groups of 4, Round of 32 knockout
> Hosts: USA / Canada / Mexico | Final: July 19 at MetLife Stadium

---

## Group A

### Mexico 🇲🇽 — FIFA #15 · CONCACAF · HOST
- **World Cup appearances:** 17 (first: 1930) — most in CONCACAF history
- **Best result:** Quarter-finals (1970, 1986 — both as hosts!)
- **Last WC:** Round of 32 2022 (lost to Argentina)
- **Recent form:** 4W 1D 1L in last 6; scored 10, conceded 5. Home advantage significant.
- **Key players:** Santiago Gimenez (AC Milan, ST), Hirving Lozano (Club América, RW), Edson Álvarez (West Ham, CDM)
- **Attack:** 1.6 avg | **Defense:** 1.1 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Enormous home-crowd advantage at Estadio Azteca and Estadio Akron. Gimenez is a clinical Serie A striker. Álvarez anchors midfield.
- **Weaknesses:** Historical knockout curse — have never advanced past R16 at any non-home WC. Lozano aging; depth thin beyond the big three.
- **Outlook:** Group A favorites by Elo and home advantage. Expect to advance comfortably; the real test is R32 onwards.

### South Africa 🇿🇦 — FIFA #60 · CAF
- **World Cup appearances:** 4 (first: 1998; hosted 2010)
- **Best result:** Group Stage (1998, 2002, 2010 host); R16 2010
- **Last WC:** 2002 Group Stage
- **Recent form:** 3W 2D 2L in last 7; qualified through CAF with physicality and pace.
- **Key players:** Percy Tau (Al Ahly, AM), Themba Zwane (Mamelodi Sundowns, AM), Ronwen Williams (Mamelodi Sundowns, GK)
- **Attack:** 1.2 avg | **Defense:** 1.4 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Passionate and physical. Williams is an elite goalkeeper. Tau provides creativity.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited technical quality vs top sides. Front line inconsistent.
- **Outlook:** Likely to finish 3rd or 4th. Could cause an upset in the opener vs Mexico if fired up.

### South Korea 🇰🇷 — FIFA #25 · AFC
- **World Cup appearances:** 11 (first: 1954) — most in AFC
- **Best result:** 4th place 2002 (co-host)
- **Last WC:** Round of 16 2022 (beat Portugal in groups)
- **Recent form:** 4W 2D 1L; Lee Kang-in led Asian qualifying with 8 goals.
- **Key players:** Son Heung-min (Tottenham, LW/ST — captain), Lee Kang-in (PSG, CAM — UCL winner 2025), Kim Min-jae (Bayern Munich, CB)
- **Attack:** 1.4 avg | **Defense:** 1.2 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Son is a top-20 global player. Lee Kang-in won the UCL with PSG. Kim Min-jae is one of the world's best center-backs.
- **Weaknesses:** Over-reliance on Son for goals. Depth behind the star trio is limited.
- **Outlook:** Strong contender for 2nd place in Group A. A Son-Lee Kang-in partnership is dangerous.

### Czechia 🇨🇿 — FIFA #41 · UEFA
- **World Cup appearances:** 9 (as Czechoslovakia: 1934 finalist, 1962 finalist; as Czech Republic: 2006)
- **Best result:** Runner-up 1934, 1962 (as Czechoslovakia)
- **Last WC:** 2006 Group Stage
- **Recent form:** 3W 2D 2L; qualified through UEFA playoffs.
- **Key players:** Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen, ST), Tomas Soucek (West Ham, CM), Vladimir Coufal (West Ham, RB)
- **Attack:** 1.3 avg | **Defense:** 1.2 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Schick is a proven Bundesliga striker. Soucek provides energy and aerial threat. Solid West Ham connection.
- **Weaknesses:** Squad depth is shallow. Aging core around Soucek/Coufal.
- **Outlook:** Competitive in Group A but likely fighting with South Africa for 3rd place.

## Group B

### Canada 🇨🇦 — FIFA #30 · CONCACAF · HOST
- **World Cup appearances:** 2 (1986, 2022)
- **Best result:** Group Stage (1986, 2022)
- **Last WC:** 2022 Group Stage (first appearance in 36 years)
- **Recent form:** 4W 2D 1L in last 7; scored 14 in qualifying final round.
- **Key players:** Alphonso Davies (Bayern Munich, LB/LW — world-class), Jonathan David (Lille, ST — top scorer), Stephen Eustaquio (Porto, CM)

- **Weaknesses:** Limited depth beyond the top 3 stars. Defensive questions beyond Davies.

### Qatar 🇶🇦 — FIFA #95 · AFC

### Switzerland 🇨🇭 — FIFA #19 · UEFA

- **Weaknesses:** Lacks a world-class striker. Can be too rigid vs teams that press high.
- **Outlook:** Co-favorites with Canada to win Group B. Strong knockout potential.

## Group C

### Brazil 🇧🇷 — FIFA #6 · CONMEBOL
- **World Cup appearances:** 23 (first: 1930) — most ever, only country to qualify for all WCs
- **Best result:** Champions (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002) — record 5 titles
- **Last WC:** Quarter-finals 2022 (lost to Croatia on penalties)
- **Recent form:** 5W 1D 1L in last 7; beat Croatia 3-1 and demolished Panama 6-2 in friendlies. Now managed by Carlo Ancelotti.
- **Key players:** Vinicius Jr (Real Madrid, LW — Ballon d'Or 2024), Rodrygo (Real Madrid, RW), Raphinha (Barcelona, AM), Bruno Guimaraes (Newcastle, CM)
- **Attack:** 2.0 avg | **Defense:** 1.0 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Ancelotti brings World-class tactical organization. Vinicius is the most feared winger on the planet. Raphinha adds creativity; Bruno Guimaraes controls tempo.
- **Weaknesses:** No world-class #9 since Neymar era ended. Defensive lapses on transitions remain.
- **Outlook:** Group C is Brazil's to win. One of the tournament's top 3 favorites overall.

### Morocco 🇲🇦 — FIFA #8 · CAF
- **World Cup appearances:** 8 (first: 1970)
- **Best result:** 4th place 2022 — historic first African semi-final
- **Last WC:** 4th place 2022 (beat Spain on penalties; beat Portugal; lost to France in SF)
- **Recent form:** 4W 3D 0L in last 7; conceded just 3 goals in 7 matches.
- **Key players:** Achraf Hakimi (PSG, RB — one of world's best), Brahim Diaz (Real Madrid, AM), Ayoub El Kaabi (Atletico Madrid, ST), Noussair Mazraoui (Man City, LB)
- **Attack:** 1.2 avg | **Defense:** 0.8 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Elite defensive organization — second-best defense avg in tournament alongside Ecuador/Spain. Hakimi is world-class. Deep midfield spine with European-based players.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited attacking upside — reliant on moments of individual brilliance rather than fluent play.
- **Outlook:** Second in Group C is Morocco's floor. A legitimate dark horse to reach the final four again.

### Haiti 🇭🇹 — FIFA #102 · CONCACAF
- **World Cup appearances:** 2 (1974, 2026)
- **Best result:** Group Stage 1974
- **Last WC:** 1974 (52 years ago!)
- **Recent form:** 3W 1D 3L; qualified through CONCACAF — remarkable achievement given political instability. Beat New Zealand 4-0 pre-tournament.
- **Key players:** Jean-Ricner Bellegarde (Wolves, CM), Wilson Isidor (Sunderland, ST), Duckens Nazon (Montpellier, ST — 44 international goals, all-time top scorer)
- **Attack:** 1.0 avg | **Defense:** 1.8 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** Spirited, physical, fast on the break. Nazon provides goals. Bellegarde is a Premier League regular.
- **Weaknesses:** Weakest side in Group C by a large margin. Defensive record is concerning.
- **Outlook:** Group stage exit expected. The achievement is qualifying. Any point earned would be historic.

### Scotland 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴 — FIFA #36 · UEFA
- **World Cup appearances:** 9 (first: 1954); absent since 1998
- **Best result:** Group Stage (never advanced; famous for group stage exits)
- **Last WC:** 1998 Group Stage
- **Recent form:** 5W 1D 1L in last 7 — strong qualifying campaign under Steve Clarke. Scott McTominay's bicycle kick vs Denmark sealed qualification.
- **Key players:** Scott McTominay (Napoli, CM — talisman), Andrew Robertson (Liverpool, LB), Kieran Tierney (Real Sociedad, LB), Ryan Christie (Bournemouth, AM)
- **Attack:** 1.4 avg | **Defense:** 1.3 conceded avg
- **Strengths:** McTominay has elevated to world-class at Napoli. Robertson is still elite at Liverpool. Physical and organized.
- **Weaknesses:** Missing Billy Gilmour (injury concern). No world-class striker. 28-year absence from WC is significant.
- **Outlook:** Battle with Morocco for 2nd place. Scotland's best chance since the 1990s.

## Group E

### Germany 🇩🇪 — FIFA #10 · UEFA
- **World Cup appearances:** 21 (first: 1934) — tied with Brazil for most ever
- **Best result:** Champions (1954, 1974, 1990, 2014)
- **Last WC:** Group Stage 2022 — shock exit as defending champions
- **Recent form:** 5W 0D 1L in last 6 — outstanding form. Scored 18 goals in 5-game winning streak. Hosted Euro 2024 (semi-final exit vs Spain).

- **Weaknesses:** No classic #9 striker (Havertz plays false 9). Lack of experience in WC knockout games since 2014.
- **Outlook:** Clear favorites to win Group E and one of the tournament's top 4 contenders.

### Ivory Coast 🇨🇮 — FIFA #34 · CAF

- **Weaknesses:** Can be exposed by teams that disrupt their defensive shape. Limited WC experience.
- **Outlook:** The tournament's most underrated team. Legitimate dark horse to advance from Group E. A goal-fest vs Germany is the decisive match.

### Ecuador 🇪🇨 — FIFA #23 · CONMEBOL
- **World Cup appearances:** 4 (2002, 2006, 2014, 2022)

- **Last WC:** 2022 Group Stage
- **Recent form:** Strongest defense in CONMEBOL qualifying — conceded only 5 goals in 18 matches.

- **Outlook:** Can challenge for 2nd in Group E. Caicedo vs Musiala/Wirtz is a fascinating tactical battle.
