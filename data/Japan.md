## Tournament Position
**Group C:** Japan, Brazil, Morocco, Haiti

Kubo's emergence as world-class winger (Real Sociedad, 24) provides hope, but without creative support (Minamino), attacking depth is questionable. The narrative: "Can Japan overcome injuries and Kubo still shine?" Moriyasu's technical approach remains but execution is compromised.
