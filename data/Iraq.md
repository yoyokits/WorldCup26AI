# Iraq 🇮🇶 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Iraq's only previous WC appearance was 1986 in Mexico — 40 years ago. They were eliminated at the group stage having never scored a goal. Since then, regional AFC competition has been their main stage, where they have been more competitive. Recent years saw improved AFC qualification performances and a strong regional identity under national team investment.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.1 avg attack / 1.4 defense in qualifying. Disciplined defensively; attack is limited to set pieces and individual moments.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Hussein is an aerial threat from corners and free kicks.
- **Style:** Compact defensive shape, quick vertical transition when winning ball.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Aymen Hussein (Al-Naft, ST):** Iraq's all-time top scorer with 67 international goals. Domestic prowess is exceptional; test will be vs French/Norwegian defending quality.
- **Amjed Attwan (Al-Shorta, CM):** Organizer and technical fulcrum of the midfield.
- **Hussein Ali (Al-Zawra, RW):** Iraq's most dynamic wide player, provides width and crosses.
- **Key note:** All key players are domestic Iraq-based — limited European-level experience outside of occasional friendlies against Asian competition.
- **Tactic:** 4-4-2 defensive shape, direct to Hussein, physical battles.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs France:** No realistic result expected. France should win comfortably.
- **vs Norway:** Haaland + Sorloth vs Iraq's defense — highest-risk match for goals conceded.
- **vs Senegal:** The best opportunity. Mane + Koulibaly vs Hussein + defense. The competitive Group I fixture.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Iraq's return to the WC for the first time since 1986 is a major achievement for Middle Eastern football. Realistically, they are the weakest team in Group I and a group stage exit is expected. However, if Hussein can replicate his domestic form and the defensive block holds for 70+ minutes vs Senegal, a draw is not impossible. The result vs Senegal defines Iraq's WC narrative.
