# Ecuador 🇪🇨 — 2026 World Cup Squad & Analysis

## Tournament Position
**Group E:** Ecuador, Germany, Curaçao, Ivory Coast
**Recent Success:** CONMEBOL Copa América participant

## Historical Arc
Ecuador's World Cup history: first appearance 2002, then consistent qualification (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022). Small nation of 18 million competes against giants. CONMEBOL qualification required. Now under coach Sebastián Beccacece with emphasis on defensive solidity and counter-attacking.

## 2026 Squad & Key Players
- **Sebastián Beccacece (Coach)** — Defensive organization specialist
- **Goalkeeper:** Moisés Ramírez or Alexander Domínguez
- **Defenders:** Pervis Estupiñán (Villarreal, young left-back), Piero Hincapié (Bayer Leverkusen, center-back), Félix Torres, Byron Castillo
- **Midfield:** Moisés Caicedo (Chelsea, star midfielder, Uruguay-switched to Ecuador, dynamic engine), Janer Montoya, Alan Franco
- **Forwards:** Enner Valencia (Pachuca, captain, 49 international goals all-time, oldest star), Anthony Valencia, Kendry Páez (young prospect)

## Recent Form & Qualifying
- CONMEBOL qualifying: Qualified; Caicedo emergence as Chelsea star provides midfielder depth
- Squad composition: Small nation relying on selective European talent (Caicedo, Estupiñán, Hincapié) + domestic players
- Enner Valencia: 49 goals but aging (33), fitness questions
