# Czechia 🇨🇿 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
As Czechoslovakia: WC runners-up in 1934 and 1962 — genuine historical pedigree. As the Czech Republic / Czechia since 1993: consistent qualifiers but never recaptured that elite level. Best modern WC result: 2006 group stage. Euro 2004 semi-finals remain their modern peak.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.3 avg attack / 1.2 defense in recent qualifying. Disciplined but not explosive.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Soucek and Schick both strong aerial threats from set pieces — above-average corner delivery.
- **Possession Style:** Organized, medium-block pressing. Transition-focused under Jaroslav Silhavy's replacement system.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen, ST):** Prolific Bundesliga striker — 28+ goals/season at Leverkusen. Clinical finisher in box.
- **Tomas Soucek (West Ham, CM):** Engine of Czech midfield. Box-to-box runner, headers goals, engine of energy.
- **Vladimir Coufal (West Ham, RB):** Consistent PL performer, excellent overlapping runs, crosses well.
- **Tactic:** 4-2-3-1 with deep block, quick transition to Schick.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Mexico:** No WC history. Expect physical, direct battle with set-piece focus.
- **vs South Korea:** No WC history. Lee Kang-in vs Soucek/Coufal will be decisive midfield battle.
- **vs South Africa:** Physical, direct African style could trouble Czechia's aging defense.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Czechia enters as the group's most tactically organized outside-top-2 side. Schick's ability to win headers and hold up play is valuable against physical opponents. The West Ham connection (Soucek + Coufal) provides Premier League quality in the spine. Group A ceiling: 3rd place, but an upset of South Korea is realistic if set pieces land.
