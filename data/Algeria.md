# Algeria 🇩🇿 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Algeria's WC highlight: 2014 Round of 16 — pushed Germany to extra time (lost 2-1 aet), widely regarded as one of the best group-stage underdog performances ever. 1982 WC: beat West Germany 2-1 in one of the biggest upsets in history. AFCON champions in 2019. Now returning after 12-year absence — AFCON 2023 group exit raised questions, but CAF qualifying qualification silenced critics.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.4 avg attack / 1.2 defense. Balanced, physical, technical Algerian style.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Mahrez's free-kick quality is elite. Bennacer's delivery from deep.
- **Style:** 4-3-3 pressing game under Petkovic, with Mahrez as the free-roaming creative outlet.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Riyad Mahrez (Al-Ahli, RW — captain):** 35 but still playing at highest level in Saudi Pro League. Mahrez on form is one of the most effective wingers in world football — one-touch finishing, dribbling, set pieces. AFCON 2019 winner, PL champion (Man City 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023).
- **Ismael Bennacer (AC Milan, CM):** The midfield engine. Physical, technical, Champions League regular. Injury history is a concern.
- **Youcef Atal (Nice, RB):** Dynamic right-back, can play as winger. Explosive pace.
- **Adam Ounas (Nice, AM):** Creative spark off the bench.
- **Tactic:** 4-3-3 with Mahrez as right winger with freedom to drift inside.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Argentina:** Algeria held West Germany for 90 minutes in 1982 (won 2-1) — historical underdog precedent exists.
- **vs Austria:** The critical Group J battle for 2nd place. Mahrez vs Sabitzer/Laimer.
- **vs Jordan:** Algeria should win this match comfortably with quality advantage.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Algeria's 2014 performance vs Germany remains a template — organized defense, rapid counters, Mahrez brilliance. If Bennacer is fit and Mahrez is in form, Algeria can give Austria a genuine contest for 2nd place. The AFCON 2023 failure is concerning context but CAF qualifying showed improvement. Ceiling: Round of 32. Floor: group stage exit if key injuries occur. Everything runs through Mahrez — if he's fit, Algeria are dangerous.
