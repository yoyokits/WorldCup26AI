# Bosnia & Herzegovina 🇧🇦 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Only two WC appearances: 2014 (group stage, debut) and now 2026. Despite having Edin Dzeko — one of Eastern Europe's all-time greatest strikers — Bosnia have never escaped the group stage. History is thin but the squad has matured since 2014 and UEFA Nations League experience has raised their competitive level.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.3 avg attack / 1.5 defense. Tend to concede more than comfortable in tighter tournaments.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Dzeko and Kolasinac provide good aerial presence. Corners dangerous.
- **Style:** Direct, physical, reliant on Dzeko's hold-up play and wide players' crossing.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Edin Dzeko (Fenerbahçe, ST — captain):** At 38, still a crafty striker with elite touch and movement. All-time BH top scorer with 63 goals. Provides experience and leadership.
- **Sead Kolasinac (Marseille, LB):** Physical overlapping full-back. Provides width and crosses.
- **Amer Gojak (Trabzonspor, CM):** Box-to-box energy player, will need to cover for defensive lapses.
- **Tactic:** 4-3-3 or 4-1-4-1 depending on opponent. Direct balls to Dzeko.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Canada (2014 WC):** No head-to-head at WC. Canada as hosts will have crowd support — energy battle.
- **vs Switzerland:** UEFA opponents — Switzerland's discipline will suffocate Dzeko's movement.
- **vs Qatar:** Best winnable match. Physical dominance should lead to a result here.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Bosnia's best hope is a draw vs Canada and a win vs Qatar. The aging squad is at a crossroads — Dzeko's final major tournament performance will define how the generation is remembered. If Gojak and younger players step up, Bosnia could be competitive for 3rd place. Otherwise, group stage exit.
