### Key Facts
- First World Cup hosted by **three nations** simultaneously
- First men's World Cup since 2002 to be co-hosted by multiple countries
- Mexico becomes the **first country to host/co-host three Men's World Cups** (1970, 1986, 2026)
- Canada makes its **debut** as a men's World Cup host
- Returns to traditional Northern Hemisphere summer schedule after Qatar 2022's November/December format

## 🏆 Format & Structure

### Group Stage
- **12 groups (A–L)** of 4 teams each
- Each team plays **3 group matches**
- Top 2 from each group + **8 best third-placed teams** advance to Round of 32

### Knockout Stage (New Format)
```
Round of 32 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Third Place Match → Final
   32 teams      16 teams        8 teams          2 teams       2 teams
```
- **Total matches per finalist:** 8 (up from 7 in previous tournaments)
- Tournament lasts **39 days** (vs. 32 days in 2014/2018)

```
Group Stage (12 groups × 3 matches = 36 group matches per matchday over 2 weeks)
    │
    ├── Round of 32: 16 matches (July 1–6)

    │
    ├── Quarterfinals: 4 matches (July 5–8)
    │
    ├── Semifinals: 2 matches (July 9–10)
    │   └── Losers play in Third Place Match (July 14)
    │
    └── Final: July 19 at MetLife Stadium, New Jersey
```

## 📊 Qualification Summary

| Confederation | Qualification Period | Key Facts |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **CONMEBOL** | Sep 2023 – Nov 2025 | Traditional round-robin; first qualifier (Sep 7, 2023) |
| **UEFA** | Mar–Nov 2025 + Playoffs | 12 group winners auto-qualify; runners-up in playoffs |
| **AFC** | Oct 2023 – Mar 2026 | 8 direct spots + 1 playoff slot (first time for AFC) |
| **CAF** | Sep 2023 – Nov 2025 | 9 direct spots; draw held July 13, 2023 in Abidjan |
| **CONCACAF** | Sep 2023 – Oct 2025 | Hex format for non-hosts; hosts auto-qualify |
| **OFC** | Mar–Jun 2025 | First guaranteed berth in World Cup history! |

### Intercontinental Play-offs (March 26 & 31, 2026)
- Held in **Guadalajara and Monterrey, Mexico**
- Format: Bottom 4 FIFA-ranked teams play single-elimination → winners face top 2 teams
- 2 spots remaining decided through this tournament
