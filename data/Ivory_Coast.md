# Ivory Coast 🇨🇮 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Three previous WC appearances (2006, 2010, 2014) — all group stage exits but competitive performances. Famous for the "group of death" 2010 (with Brazil, Portugal, North Korea). AFCON history is richer: winners in 1992, 2015, and dramatically in 2024 on home soil. The 2024 AFCON win was a redemption story — eliminated from group stage then rallied to win the tournament.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.5 avg attack / 0.7 conceded avg. Outstanding defensive record in CAF qualifying — ZERO goals conceded in 10 matches.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Haller provides excellent aerial platform. Kessie's delivery from deep is dangerous.
- **Style:** Organized, aggressive pressing with rapid wing transitions. Beat France 2-1 in a 2026 pre-tournament friendly.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Sebastien Haller (Borussia Dortmund, ST):** Clinical target striker who overcame testicular cancer in 2022 to return stronger. Aerial dominance, hold-up play, goals (18+ in Bundesliga seasons).
- **Simon Adingra (Brighton, RW):** Explosive pace, one-vs-one dribbler, strong Premier League season with Brighton (15 goals in 2024-25).
- **Franck Kessie (Barcelona, CM — captain):** Physical, powerful defensive midfielder with Champions League experience at Barcelona.
- **Seko Fofana (Al-Nassr, CM):** Box-to-box energy. Influential in French football before Saudi move.
- **Tactic:** 4-3-3 high press, Adingra and one other winger stretching defenses for Haller.
- **Key fact:** Did not concede a single goal in 10 CAF qualifying matches — the best defensive record of any qualifier.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Germany:** The decisive Group E match. Ivory Coast beat France 2-1 pre-WC — Germany is a comparable level. Could be a classic.
- **vs Ecuador:** Battle between two organized, defensive-minded sides. Set pieces likely decisive.
- **vs Curaçao:** Should win comfortably.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
The most underrated team in the tournament. AFCON 2024 champions, zero goals conceded in CAF qualifying, and a pre-tournament win over France. Ivory Coast in Group E is playing above their FIFA ranking — the actual quality is closer to top 20. If Haller is fit and Adingra performs, a quarter-final run is not unrealistic. The decisive question: can they handle Germany's pressing intensity?
