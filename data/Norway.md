# Norway 🇳🇴 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Norway's WC history: 4 appearances (1938, 1994, 1998, 2026). Famous 1994 appearance where they held Brazil to a 1-0 group win (Rekdal goal). Beat Brazil! 1998 was their best modern showing. Then 28 years of near-misses, most famously failing to qualify despite Haaland's extraordinary form — until 2026 when Haaland's 16 qualifying goals carried them through.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.8 avg attack / 1.1 defense. Attack is heavily Haaland-driven — he scored 16 of Norway's European qualifying goals.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Haaland's aerial dominance makes corners and free kicks disproportionately dangerous.
- **Style:** Direct, physical, built around Haaland. Odegaard provides the creative link. Not possession-heavy.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Erling Haaland (Man City, ST):** The most lethal striker in world football. 16 goals in European qualifying (most of any player in Europe). Champions League top scorer. If Haaland fires, Norway will score against anyone.
- **Martin Odegaard (Arsenal, CAM — captain):** The creative brain. One of the PL's best playmakers — vision, ball retention, key passes. Haaland-Odegaard is the most dangerous partnership in Group I.
- **Alexander Sorloth (Atletico Madrid, ST):** World-class backup striker. Having BOTH Haaland AND Sorloth is unprecedented luxury for Norway.
- **Andreas Christensen (Barcelona, CB):** Elite center-back anchoring the defense.
- **Tactic:** 4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1. Odegaard behind Haaland. Direct balls to Haaland's runs.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs France:** A genuine contest. Haaland vs Varane/Upamecano. France should win but Norway can score here.
- **vs Senegal:** The key qualifying match. Haaland vs Koulibaly is a massive battle.
- **vs Iraq:** Norway should win comfortably — Haaland hat-trick territory.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Norway's first WC in 28 years, powered by a supernatural striker. Haaland arriving at a WC is the tournament's biggest "what if" — can he replicate club form at international level? He scored 16 qualifying goals suggesting yes. With Odegaard creating and Sorloth as backup, Norway can legitimately reach the Round of 16 if they beat Senegal or Iraq and take a point vs France. Dark horse status is justified.
