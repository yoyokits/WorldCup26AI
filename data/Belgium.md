# Belgium 🇧🇪 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Belgium's "Golden Generation" has been building since the early 2010s. 3rd place in 2018 — their best WC result — was supposed to be the launchpad to a title, but 2022 ended in a traumatic group-stage-level quarter-final exit (technically QF but felt like underachievement). Euro 2024 was also disappointing. Now at their twilight — De Bruyne 34, Lukaku 31, Vertonghen retired, Hazard retired. This is likely the final chapter.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.7 avg attack / 1.1 defense. Courtois's 13 clean sheets in La Liga 2024-25 underscore elite defensive record.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** De Bruyne's delivery is world-class. Belgium average 3.1 dangerous corners per match.
- **Style:** Direct vertical passing through De Bruyne, with Lukaku providing physical target for 2nd balls and Trossard cutting inside.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Kevin De Bruyne (Man City, CM — captain):** Still top 3 midfielder globally at 34. Direct passing, vision, set-piece delivery. If fit throughout, Belgium are a genuine top-8 team.
- **Thibaut Courtois (Real Madrid, GK):** La Liga's best goalkeeper in 2024-25 — 13 clean sheets, commanding in box and distribution.
- **Romelu Lukaku (Galatasaray, ST):** Physical target striker. 85 international goals, 2nd all-time Belgium scorer. Hit form at Galatasaray after difficult Chelsea/Napoli years.
- **Leandro Trossard (Arsenal, LW):** In-form Arsenal wide player, consistent PL performer.
- **Amadou Onana (Aston Villa, CDM):** Powerful defensive midfielder, likely to screen for De Bruyne's runs.
- **Tactic:** 4-2-3-1 or 3-4-3. De Bruyne free role, Onana screens, Courtois sweeps everything up.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Egypt:** The most interesting Group G match — Salah vs De Bruyne will define Group G. Belgium have historical edge over African teams.
- **vs Iran:** Belgium should be too strong tactically but Iran's organization will make it difficult.
- **vs New Zealand:** Expect a comfortable win to start the tournament.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Belgium's final opportunity. The Golden Generation has consistently underdelivered relative to squad quality — they've never won a major tournament despite being ranked #1 globally for 3 years (2015-2018). De Bruyne, Courtois, and Lukaku all know this is the last dance. Psychological pressure is enormous. If they can manage the emotional weight, a semi-final run is entirely realistic. Their Elo (1940) undervalues them slightly — De Bruyne's presence adds 1-2% championship probability alone.
