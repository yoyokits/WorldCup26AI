# DR Congo 🇨🇩 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
DR Congo's only previous WC appearance was as Zaire in 1974 — famous for 9-0 thrashing by Yugoslavia and infamous for Mwepu Ilunga kicking the ball from a free kick. The 2026 qualification is thus their second-ever WC appearance and first in 52 years. CAF history is stronger: Congo DR (as Zaïre) won the AFCON in 1968 and 1974; DR Congo won it again in 2022. A footballing nation finally back at the sport's biggest stage.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.2 avg attack / 1.3 defense. Solid but not dominant in CAF qualifying.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Mbemba provides aerial dominance at corners. Wissa is a clinical finisher from close range.
- **Style:** Physical, direct, 4-4-2 structure. Work rate and resilience.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Yoane Wissa (Newcastle, ST):** Performing at Champions League-level with Newcastle. Dynamic, direct, clinical finisher. His Newcastle form (15+ goals in 2024-25) is the squad's best individual performance.
- **Chancel Mbemba (Genoa, CB — captain):** 100+ caps, experienced European defender. Organizes defensive shape.
- **Cédric Bakambu (Marseille, ST):** Ligue 1 experience at Marseille — backup striker quality.
- **Tactic:** 4-4-2 with Wissa leading a high press. Mbemba + partner CB form solid block.
- **Pre-WC note:** Drew 0-0 with Denmark (FIFA #20) in a friendly — suggests defensive organization is credible.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Portugal:** No realistic result. Contain Ronaldo and Bruno Fernandes.
- **vs Colombia:** The key Group K match. Wissa vs Colombia's defense is the key battle.
- **vs Uzbekistan:** Most competitive match — similar Elo levels. This defines Congo DR's group stage.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
DR Congo's 52-year return to the WC is historic. With Wissa in excellent form and Mbemba providing defensive experience, they are competitive with Uzbekistan. Drawing 0-0 with Denmark pre-WC suggests legitimate improvement. Realistic target: beat Uzbekistan, take a point from Colombia. Group stage exit expected but a credible performance to be proud of. Wissa's Newcastle form gives them at least one guaranteed threat to any defense.
