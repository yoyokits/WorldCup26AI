# Haiti 🇭🇹 — Historical Tournament Analysis & Trends

## Historical Performance Arc
Haiti's only WC appearance before 2026 was 1974 — 52 years ago. Their 1974 run was notable for Emmanuel Sanon's famous goal past Sepp Maier (breaking German GK's shutout streak), but they were eliminated in the group stage. The 2026 qualification is thus their second-ever WC appearance and most significant footballing achievement in 50 years, especially given the severe political and security challenges in the country.

## Statistical Trends
- **Goals Scored/Conceded:** 1.0 avg attack / 1.8 defense in qualifying. Goals come in bursts; defense is fragile.
- **Set-Piece Efficiency:** Limited delivery quality but some aerial presence from Nazon and Isidor.
- **Style:** Counter-attacking, physical, fast transitions. Not possession-heavy.

## Key Players & Tactical Notes
- **Duckens Nazon (Montpellier, ST):** All-time Haiti top scorer with 44 international goals. Pace and clinical finishing.
- **Jean-Ricner Bellegarde (Wolves, CM):** The highest-profile European-based player — brings Premier League physicality and intelligence to midfield.
- **Wilson Isidor (Sunderland, ST):** Pacey forward, effective on the counter.
- **Qualif. note:** Played home games in Curaçao (neutral venue) due to ongoing political crisis in Haiti.
- **Tactic:** 4-4-2 deep block, long balls to Nazon on transitions.

## Matchup History & Tendencies
- **vs Brazil:** No WC head-to-head. Brazil will dominate possession; Haiti must hold deep and find counter-moments.
- **vs Morocco:** Morocco's physicality and tactical discipline will be challenging to break down.
- **vs Scotland:** Best realistic winnable match. Both sides at similar levels despite ranking gap.

## Analyst Verdict for 2026
Haiti's presence is historically significant. They are the weakest team in Group C by Elo, but the spirit and athleticism they bring can produce upset moments. A point vs Scotland is their realistic target. Every minute they are competitive is a win for Caribbean football. Bellegarde's Premier League experience will be crucial in organizing the midfield. Beat New Zealand 4-0 pre-tournament — that form is encouraging.
